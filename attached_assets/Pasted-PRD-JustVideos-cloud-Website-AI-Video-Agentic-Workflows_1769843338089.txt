PRD: JustVideos.cloud Website (AI + Video + Agentic Workflows)
1) Product summary

JustVideos.cloud is the public-facing website for a modern video production house and AI workflow studio offering:

End-to-end video production (brand, corporate, product, safety, training, explainers, event, social, reels).

Generative AI services (image/video generation, voice, script, storyboards, dubbing, localization).

Agentic AI services (voice agents, business workflows, AI websites, custom apps, automations).

Custom-built workflows for enterprises and SMEs: lead capture → auto follow-up → content generation → approvals → publishing.

Website goals:

Convert visitors into qualified leads (book a call / request quote / submit brief).

Prove credibility (brands served: L&T, Tata Steel, ITC, Emami, Haldia Petrochemicals, GRSE, etc.).

Explain “what we do” simply, with strong examples and packaged offers.

Support productized services (templates/packages) + custom enterprise engagements.

2) Problem statement

Potential clients struggle to understand:

What “Generative AI” and “Agentic AI” means in business outcomes.

Whether results are enterprise-grade (security, timelines, approvals, compliance).

How much it costs and what process looks like.

The breadth: video + AI + automation + apps.

The current market has:

Traditional production houses (slow + expensive).

AI freelancers (fast but inconsistent, weak governance).

Agencies (good branding but weak in technical automation).

JustVideos should position as: “Production + AI engineering + workflow automation under one roof.”

3) Target users & personas
A) Enterprise Marketing/Brand Head

Needs: high-quality videos at scale, brand safety, approvals, vendor reliability.
Success: on-time delivery, consistent brand output, measurable campaign outputs.

B) Plant/Operations/Safety Head (Industrial)

Needs: safety/training videos, multilingual SOP content, compliance documentation.
Success: reduced incidents, faster onboarding, consistent training.

C) SME Founder/Marketing Manager

Needs: cost-effective brand videos + AI website + lead automation.
Success: more leads, better conversion, content pipeline.

D) Product/IT/Transformation Lead

Needs: agentic workflows, internal tools, voice agents, integrations.
Success: reduced operational load, measurable ROI, governance.

4) Value proposition & positioning

Primary message:
“JustVideos.cloud builds premium videos and AI-powered workflows that scale content + operations.”

Key differentiators:

Hybrid studio: Creative + Engineering + Automation

Custom workflows (n8n / agents / CRM / WhatsApp / email / approvals)

Multilingual, localization & voice

Enterprise readiness: brand governance + controlled review cycles

End-to-end delivery: script → shoot/AI → edit → versions → distribution

5) Success metrics (KPIs)
Acquisition & conversion

Site to lead conversion rate: 2–5% baseline (target), improve over time.

CTA completion rate (Book a Call / Submit Brief): >20% of CTA clicks.

Average time on key pages (Services, Workflows, Case Studies): >90 sec.

Lead quality

% qualified leads (meeting criteria): >40%

Time-to-first-response: <15 minutes (via automation + WhatsApp/email)

Trust & proof

Case study engagement rate: >25% of visitors scroll >50%

Brand logo strip interactions: measurable (optional)
