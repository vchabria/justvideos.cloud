# PRD: JustVideos.cloud Lead Generation & Outreach System

**Author:** Hemant Chabria | JustVideos.cloud
**Date:** February 2026
**Status:** Draft — for Bubble Lab to build

---

## 1. Business Context

JustVideos.cloud is a Kolkata-based video production studio offering video production, generative AI video, voice agents, agentic workflows, and AI-powered websites. Existing clients include L&T, Tata Steel, Emami, GRSE, Haldia Petrochemicals, ITC, Aditya Birla Group, Reliance, IndianOil, GAIL, JSW, Hindalco, Marico, Britannia, and Nestle.

**Goal:** Build a Bubble-based lead generation and email outreach tool that:
1. Stores and manages 280+ target companies across India
2. Auto-researches each prospect before outreach
3. Sends personalized email sequences from hemant@chabria.com
4. Tracks opens, replies, and pipeline

## 11. What NOT to Do

- Don't buy email lists — they destroy deliverability and reputation
- Don't send more than 50 cold emails/day per mailbox
- Don't skip personalization — Indian enterprise buyers delete generic emails
- Don't pitch hard in email 1 — open a conversation, offer value first
- Don't forget WhatsApp — many Indian decision makers respond faster on WhatsApp than email
- Don't ignore referral chains — one warm intro from L&T or Tata Steel is worth 100 cold emails
- Don't email personal Gmail/Yahoo addresses — always use work emails
- Don't send on weekends or after 6pm IST — Indian corporate inboxes are 9-6
- Don't use clickbait subjects — Indian enterprise buyers are skeptical of "You won't believe..."
- Don't neglect the Kolkata angle — being local is a genuine competitive advantage for site visits, reshoots, and relationship building
